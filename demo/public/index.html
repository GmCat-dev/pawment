Take crypto payments on your own server

Pawment is a free, open-source checkout you run yourself. It only watches your wallets: no keys that can spend ever touch the server, and payments go straight to you.

Try the live demo Source code Install guide

The demo is a private sandbox just for you, on pretend money. It clears itself after an hour of no use.

Watch-only

Paste a public key or address. Pawment hands out fresh addresses from it, sees payments arrive, and never holds anything that can move your coins.

Light

One small Node program, no blockchain node, no database server. It reads public blockchain services and checks each payment with a second one.

Fair to buyers

Wallet "Pay" buttons and QR codes, small differences forgiven, underpaid orders stay open for the rest, overpayments listed for refund, late payments get a grace period.

Coins

  • Cardano ADA, any Cardano token, USDCx, USDM, USDA, DJED, iUSD
  • Bitcoin family BTC, LTC, BCH, Groestlcoin
  • Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon ETH, BNB, POL, USDT, USDC
  • TRON TRX, USDT
  • Monero XMR (fresh subaddress per order, view key only)

How it works

  1. Your site asks for an order with one API call (amount in dollars) and sends the buyer to the checkout link.
  2. The buyer picks a coin and pays from their wallet. The price is held for 30 minutes.
  3. Pawment sees it on the blockchain, waits for enough confirmations, and tells your site with a signed notice.

You get a dashboard with orders, payments to check by hand, refunds (you send them yourself), per-coin discounts and a ledger export for taxes.
